AI-Driven Smart City Service Management using Predictive Analytics and Reinforcement Learning

Dr. Ismail El Sayad • School of Computing, University of the Fraser Valley

Developed a reinforcement learning framework that proactively allocates municipal service crews using demand forecasting and sequential decision making across Vancouver's 311 service system.

  • Built a PPO-based dispatch agent trained on approximately 1.18 million City of Vancouver 311 records.
  • Integrated XGBoost demand forecasting directly into the RL environment.
  • Designed a scalable priority-vector action space for crew allocation.
  • Evaluated against multiple baseline dispatch policies across paired simulations.

Multi-View 3D Tree Localization from UAV Imagery Using Segmentation and Ray Triangulation

Dr. Kongwen Frank Zhang • School of Computing, University of the Fraser Valley

Developed a computer vision pipeline for localizing trees from multi-view UAV imagery using segmentation, structure-from-motion, and least-squares geometric triangulation.

  • Combined SAM2 segmentation with COLMAP camera reconstruction.
  • Localized trees across multiple viewpoints without GPS camera poses.
  • Built an end-to-end workflow compatible with Google Colab.
